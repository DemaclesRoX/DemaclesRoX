Heatwave Alert: Poultry Management

Published: May 12, 2026 | Category: Poultry

With temperatures rising above 38°C in northern districts, broiler mortality risk is high. Increase ventilation, add Vitamin C to water during noon, and avoid feeding during peak heat hours (12 PM - 4 PM).

Published: April 28, 2026 | Category: Agriculture

Farmers in Bogura and Rajshahi are reporting Fall Armyworm infestations in early maize crops. Apply recommended bio-pesticides early. Check our integrated pest management guide for details.

Pond Water Quality Before Monsoon

Published: April 15, 2026 | Category: Fisheries

Prepare your aquaculture ponds for the upcoming monsoon rains. Apply slaked lime (CaOH2) as per standard guidelines to prevent sudden pH drops and oxygen depletion.
